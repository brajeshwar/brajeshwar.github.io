## Method, so this can be reproduced or argued with

1. **Every image reference in `_posts/**`** — Markdown `![]()`, `<img src>`, and the
   front-matter `image:` key. SVGs excluded.
2. **Confirmed** = the shipped file, or a same-directory master sharing its stem, carries
   `Make: Ideogram AI`; or a caption/filename declares DALL·E or `ai-generated`.
3. **Ruled out** = camera EXIF on the master (Apple, Nikon, Casio, Sony, Canon); or dated
   before DALL·E 2 opened (Jun 2022); or a stock/Unsplash attribution in the prose.
4. **Everything else is Unknown.** The shipped `.webp` files are metadata-stripped, and
   `cdn.oinam.com` strips them too (checked), so absence of metadata proves nothing.

⚠️ **Filename matches on `ai` are mostly SUBJECT, not provenance** — `mission-impossible-AI`,
`rembrandt-painting-ai`, `ai-ml-dl-llm-gai-gpt-nlp` are all about AI, not made by it. Two
early matches were also wrong on lineage and were dropped:
`/2013/the-design-of-everyday-things/` and the two Gandhi posts use files that are a
different date and a different size from the Ideogram book-shelf images they resemble.
