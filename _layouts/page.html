. Sidenotes are positioned just past the RIGHT EDGE of the measure column, so they need that column to exist as a box. Making every page full-width (2026-07-27) removed it and silently cost /books/ and /about/brajeshwar.com/ their margin notes — footnotes still rendered at the foot, which is the designed fallback, so nothing looked broken. Caught by measuring: 2 refs, 0 sidenotes. Detected from the rendered content rather than a front-matter flag, so it cannot drift out of sync with whether the page really has footnotes. ⚠️ The test is inline in the `if`, NOT hoisted into an `assign`. Liquid's `assign` takes a value or a filtered value — it does NOT evaluate operators like `contains`, and it fails silently: `assign x = content contains '…'` produced a truthy value on EVERY page, so all 22 got the class. Only `if`, `unless` and `case` evaluate `contains`. ⚠️ Trade-off: such a page is measure-width throughout, so a grid or gallery on it will not span the band. Prose pages look identical either way (the text is capped at the measure regardless), and both current users are pure prose. A page that needs both wants a .container-ideal wrapper around just the footnoted part instead — see the sidenote example in _pages/about.html. {%- endcomment -%} {%- comment -%} `class="page"` mirrors `class="post"` on a post's article, and page.css hangs its prose-measure rules off it.
