The `full:` branch is gone (2026-07-27, Brajeshwar: "for all pages, content should extend the full width of the body content area"), because there is nothing left for it to switch — wrapping the article in .container-ideal is what used to hold prose pages at 665px inside a 1024px band, and that was the inconsistency.
