The essential and perhaps everything I own. It is a layered and modular, kept monochrome, the bag it travels in, and the handful of things that stay put. It lets me shop rarely, and when I do, I buy without thinking too much, often a few of the same item at a time. I also believe in Buy Once Cry Once. The aim is for this list to get shorter with time.

And there are stories behind what I own; some of them outlived the things themselves.

Notes to Self

My life’s item should be single printable on an A4 size Paper at a readable font-size. Create a layered modular wardrobe, and stay within a monochrome color, without or as minimal/discrete branding, display of logos, and no-frills.

Stay dark; the highest color would be like black, and dark Navy Blue while Cream or Beige on the lightest end of the palette.
