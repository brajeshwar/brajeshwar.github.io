# Green Unplugged

[Culture Unplugged](https://www.cultureunplugged.com/) have released a new festival -- Green Unplugged -- with over 400 new films and a community site for film makers from the last festival. They've also added a NEWS section -- [LinkTV](https://www.cultureunplugged.com/linktv/).

Culture Unplugged has also just finished production of a mega Hollywood Movie -- [The Laundry Warrior](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1032751/) -- in partnership with LORD of the RINGs producer, 7 times Oscar, Barrie Osborne. The initiative is non-profit, for films and film makers to make a better world.
