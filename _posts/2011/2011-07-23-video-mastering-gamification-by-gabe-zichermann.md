Gabe Zichermann, aptly known as the father of gamification, is a super awesome guy. You'll learn a lot listening to his talks and he is one of the best guys to have ever talked at the [Founder Institute](https://www.founderinstitute.com/). [Gabe](https://twitter.com/gzicherm) is the author of [Game-Based Marketing](https://gamification.co/books/), founder of [gamification.co](https://gamification.co/), chair of the [Gamification Summit and Workshops](https://gsummit.com/), and the leader of the [NY Founder Institute](https://www.founderinstitute.com/).
