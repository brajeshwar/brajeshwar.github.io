# Culture Unplugged iPad Edition

Culture Unplugged have released their [iPad Application](https://www.cultureunplugged.com/film_festival_TV/documentary_ipad/) where you will discover documentaries and films from around the world from film-makers, activists, grassroots-productions and purpose driven institutions.

This program is brought to you by all participating film-makers, the festival partners and the organizing team around the world, as a philanthropic offering to uplift our collective consciousness.

The [iPad Application](https://www.cultureunplugged.com/film_festival_TV/documentary_ipad/) allows you to watch their TV Channel Live or the recorded version.
