# Founder Institute launches the 2011 Silicon Valley Semester

[Founder Institute](https://www.founderinstitute.com/) launches the next Silicon Valley semester, running from Jun 27 to Sep 27, 2011. [Apply today](https://www.founderinstitute.com/join/graduate-Brajeshwar_Oinam) and the $50 admissions test processing fee will be waived.

If you're interested in the program, you're invited to [the Grad Night Out](https://sv-gradnightout.eventbrite.com/) this Wednesday.

Note: This will be the last semester which [Adeo Ressi](https://www.linkedin.com/in/adeoressi) will personally lead.
