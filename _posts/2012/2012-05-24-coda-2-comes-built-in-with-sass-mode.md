# Coda 2 comes built-in with Sass Mode
Sometime back in Nov 2011, I was contacted by [Steven Frank](https://stevenf.com/), Co-Founder of [Panic](https://panic.com/). Panic wanted to officially support Sass with their upcoming new version of Coda, and wanted to start off from our [Sass.Mode](https://github.com/brajeshwar/Sass.mode) (maintained by me and [Tom-Marius Olsen](https://www.tommariusolsen.com/)).

Well, why not. That was an awesome thing. Today, Panic released [Coda 2](https://panic.com/coda/), complete with [Sass](https://sass-lang.com/) Mode built-in.
