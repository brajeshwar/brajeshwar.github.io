# New and Interesting Augmented Reality (ARKit) Apps for iPhones and iPads

[Robert Scoble](https://scobleizer.com/) published a [list of ARKit Apps](https://www.facebook.com/RobertScoble/posts/10155735250559655) (Augmented Reality). I found it interesting and listing it here, in no particular order, for the records. You need an ARKit-enabled iPhone or iPad to play with these apps.

1. [Sketchfab](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/sketchfab/id1201268000?mt=8) - drop 3D objects near you.
1. [Orb](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/orb/id1282295219?mt=8) - pull out basic shapes and then sculpt, rotate, and paint them to create your own Orbs.
1. [PuzzlAR: World Tour](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/puzzlar-world-tour/id1252735237?mt=8) -puzzle game.
1. [Holo](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/holo/id1194175772?mt=8) - see the quality of holograms.
1. [Army of Robots](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/army-of-robots/id1261443979&mt=8) - you must destroy the robots that are invading your REAL WORLD.
1. [Paint Space AR](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/paint-space-ar/id1260267722?mt=8) - draw in the air with all sorts of brushes and colors.
1. [Math Ninja AR](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/math-ninja-ar/id1274871322?mt=8) - get kids involved in learning math.
1. [World Brush](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/world-brush/id1277410449?mt=8) - paint with brushes on the world around you.
1. [Follow Me Dragon](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/follow-me-dragon/id1276049016?mt=8) - Say hi to your new dragon! He breathes fire, does tricks, and follows you anywhere you go!
1. [Insight Heart](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/insight-heart/id1280845473?mt=8) - explore the human heart; rotate and scale the high-resolution heart floating in front of you.
1. [Monster Park - Dino World](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/monster-park-dino-world/id1259767702?mt=8) - Would you like to make a hilarious photo with dinosaurs, those who ruled the Earth long time ago?
1. [Nitro Nation](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/nitro-nation-online/id985158853?mt=8) - For those who live life 1/4 mile at a time, Nitro Nation is the most addictive drag racing game!
1. [aCen](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/acen/id1276116927?mt=8) - a target shooting game.
1. [Fitness AR](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/fitness-ar/id1274233318?mt=8) - explore your Strava bike rides and runs in augmented reality.
1. [powARdup](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/powardup/id1271762334?mt=8)  - a fast-paced arcade survival game.
1. [Magic Sudoku](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/magic-sudoku/id1286979959?mt=8) - aim this app at a sudoku board game and it'll solve it for you.
1. [Dronetopolis](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/dronetopolis-ar/id1280548570?mt=8) - Fly a virtual drone around.
1. [Dance Reality](https://itunes.apple.com/in/app/dance-reality/id1277367395?mt=8) - Learn to dance in AR.
1. [My Very Hungry Caterpillar](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/my-very-hungry-caterpillar-ar/id1277085142?mt=8) - caterpillar game.
1. [Conduct AR](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/conduct-ar/id1256506674?mt=8) - a train game.
1. [Color BlastAR](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/color-blastar/id1277211749?mt=8) - find the paint buckets and treasure chests hidden in your room and start blasting life-sized zombies with color.
1. [AR Runner](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/ar-runner/id1275938861?mt=8) - gets you to run around.
1. [Splitter Critters](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/splitter-critters/id1107320261?mt=8) - Split the world with a swipe of your finger and then rearrange it to guide critters back to their spaceship.
1. [ARrived](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/arrived-ar-god-simulation/id1246829781?mt=8) - The player assumes the role of a deity who must lead followers through key decisions and interacts with real environment in order to guide them reach prosperity and to all kinds of crazy stuff.
1. [GIPHY World: AR GIFs+Stickers](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/giphy-world-ar-gifs-stickers/id1279438563?mt=8) - Get ready to fill the world around you with GIFs & stickers in glorious AR powered 3D!
1. [AR Sports Basketball](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/ar-sports-basketball/id1278231813?mt=8) - basketball game.
1. [AR-Basketball](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/ar-basketball/id1270144940?mt=8) - basketball game.
1. [Drive Ahead! Minigolf](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/drive-ahead-minigolf/id1268524653?mt=8) - Minigolf.
1. [Mammoth Mini Golf AR](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/mammoth-mini-golf-ar/id1267751860?mt=8) - a wacky caveman themed world of mini golf right into your home.
1. [Stack AR](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/stack-ar/id1269638287?mt=8) - Stack up the blocks as high as you can.
1. [CARROT Weather](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/carrot-weather/id961390574?mt=8) - a crazy-powerful weather app that delivers hilariously twisted forecasts.
1. [Zombie Gunship Revenant](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/zombie-gunship-revenant-ar/id1254976492?mt=8) - zombie shooter where you take control of a heavily-armed helicopter gunship and obliterate zombies from the sky.
1. [Pigeon Panic](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/pigeon-panic-ar/id1272651887?mt=8) - run haphazardly into large flocks of pigeons.
1. [Phantogeist](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/phantogeist/id1284644739?mt=8) - first person shooter game.
1. [The Machines](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/the-machines/id1280682965?mt=8) - bring an AR battlefield to life in front of your eyes.
1. [YuME: Alice's Dream](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/yume-alices-dream/id1279220869?mt=8) - a story about a dream within a dream - story, puzzles, games.
1. [Domino World](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/id1282105342) - create incredible virtual domino courses quickly and easily in the world around you, using an assortment of tiles, props and toys.
1. [shARk](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/shark-by-curiscope/id1276542911?mt=8) - lets sharks enter your world and swim with you.
1. [Twilight Pioneers:Dragon ARena](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/twilight-pioneers-dragon-arena/id1271259466?mt=8) - a story about a dragon soul controller fighting with the Dark Lord.
1. [AR Balloons](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/ar-balloons/id1286483429?mt=8) - pop Balloons in augmented reality.
1. [Knightfall](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/knightfall-ar/id1285582974?mt=8) - places you into the world of Knights Templar as they seek to defend the city of Acre from an invadaing army.
1. [The Box](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/the-box-ar/id1268182688?mt=8) - every day you get new functionality.
1. [Mancala FS5](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/mancala-fs5/id300868307?mt=8) - a board game.
