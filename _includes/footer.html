An entry whose `url` starts with "http" gets the ↗ from icons/external.svg and an `aria-label` reading "… (external link)". Both, always: the glyph is `aria-hidden`, so without the label the fact that the link leaves the site would exist only for people who can see it. ⚠️ AND NO `target`. 2026-09-01: "I don't want to break convention and force a target=_blank." Where a link opens is the reader's choice — they can ask for a new tab with a modifier-click on any link — and the mark is there so they can make that choice knowingly, not so the page can make it for them. `rel="noopener"` came off with it: with no new browsing context there is nothing for it to do. ⚠️ Same rules as the strip heading in home-strip.html, which also carries the history of the shared-named-window experiment that ran for one afternoon and was measured out of existence. Keep the two in step. {%- endcomment -%}
