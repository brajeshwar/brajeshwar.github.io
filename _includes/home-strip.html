Parameters: items the data array (site.data.books / site.data.album) title heading text href where the heading links kind "books" | "album" — picks the ratio class and the image folder intro optional paragraph, rendered above the strip inside the band captions "true" to print the item title under each thumbnail A data item may carry `media: video` or `media: audio`, overlaying a small icon so a reader can tell what the frame leads to. Omitted means a still. The heading sits in the band; the strip breaks out symmetrically around it. Both the title and every item are links. LINKS may point off-site. A `url` starting with "http" is emitted verbatim with `rel="noopener"`; anything else goes through `relative_url`. Photos link to album.oinam.com, books to posts here — and `relative_url` prepends the baseurl, so handing it an absolute URL is wrong the moment a baseurl exists. IMAGE PATHS. `img` is normally a bare filename and resolves under /static//. An `img` that already starts with "/" is used verbatim instead — which is what lets the placeholder album items borrow the book thumbnails while /static/album/ does not exist. Slice rather than `contains "/"`, because a filename in a subfolder would contain one too. THE ARROWS ARE PROGRESSIVE ENHANCEMENT. They ship as real
