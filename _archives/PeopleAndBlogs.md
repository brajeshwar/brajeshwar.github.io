# peopleandblogs.com

Manuel Moreale, hello@manuelmoreale.com

1) Let's start from the basics: can you introduce yourself? 
  
Suggestions: some background information on where you're from, what you studied or studying, how you ended up doing the job you're doing, your hobbies, things you’re passionate about, and so on.  
  
2) What's the story behind your blog?  
  
Suggestions: when you decided to start a blog, what inspired you, how did you come up with the name, how many versions you went through, the changes over the years?  
  
3) What does your creative process look like when it comes to blogging?  
  
Suggestions: where does inspiration come from, what's the writing process, do you do research, which tools do you use, how many drafts do you write, do you ask people to proof read before publishing?  
  
4) Do you have an ideal creative environment? Also do you believe the physical space influences your creativity?  
  
Suggestions: could be the overall environment, the background noise, the physical tools you're using, etc.  
  
5) A question for the techie readers: can you run us through your tech stack?   
  
Suggestions: where is the blog hosted, where is the domain registered, do you use a CMS?  
  
6) Given your experience, if you were to start a blog today, would you do anything differently?  
  
Suggestions: would you pick a different name, would you use a different platform, would you structure it differently?  
  
7) Financial question since the Web is obsessed with money: how much does it cost to run your blog? Is it just a cost, or does it generate some revenue? And what's your position on people monetising personal blogs?  
  
Suggestions: a rundown of the various costs would be great but I understand it can be a pain in the butt. For monetisation, do you like when people monetise content, what are your favorite ways of monetising a blog, do you financially support other bloggers?  
  
8) Time for some recommendations: any blog you think is worth checking out? And also, who do you think I should be interviewing next?  
  
Suggestions: go wild.  
  
9) Final question: is there anything you want to share with us?  
  
Suggestions: cool websites, books, podcasts, side projects you're working on, and so on.
