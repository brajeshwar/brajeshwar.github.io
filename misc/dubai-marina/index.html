A Walk Round Dubai Marina

Along the beach at JBR, across the Marina’s mouth, up one bank and back down the other. Dubai is hot most of the year, so the walk is a choice of hour and of side: slide the time of day to see where the towers throw their shade. Choose a stop on the model or in the list.

  1. 1 The Beach, JBR

    Where the walk starts and ends: the open square at The Beach, with the towers of JBR behind it and the sand a few steps away.

  2. 2 The beach

    The sand runs south to the Marina’s mouth, with Ain Dubai across the water on Bluewaters. There is little shade out here once the sun is up; go early, before it clears the towers behind you.

  3. 3 The Marina’s mouth

    Where the canal meets the sea. A paved path between hedges runs back toward the beach; the bridge over the mouth takes you across to the far bank.

Across the Marina’s mouth, and up the far bank

  1. 4 The south end

    The Marina Walk begins its run up the far bank, past The Jewels. Here the path forks: along the water, or up to the street behind.

  2. 5 Dubai Marina Yacht Club

    Yachts moored three deep below the towers, and on a still morning all of it twice.

  3. 6 The moorings

    The Marina Walk at its busiest: charter boats, jet skis, and cafés along the water. At night the towers light up and their lights lie broken on the water.

  4. 7 Dubai Marina Mall

    The mall on the water, and the stop for the water bus. Inside is the shade there is at noon.

  5. 8 The Marina Walk

    The promenade curls round the bays here, and the towers stand in the water upside down.

  6. 9 The bend

    The canal turns north toward Dubai Harbour. The walk crosses here and comes back down the other side.

Over the water at the bend, and back down the JBR side

  1. 10 Bay Central

    Back down the JBR side. The towers across the water are the ones you walked under; the footbridge ahead is the next way over.

  2. 11 Under the bridge

    The road bridge crosses low over the water, and its underside is the one deep shade on the walk at any hour.

  3. 12 The Walk, JBR

    The street between the towers of JBR, a block in from the beach. A few steps more and the loop is closed at The Beach.
